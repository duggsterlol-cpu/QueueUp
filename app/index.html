How do I get a Client ID?
  1. Open dev.twitch.tv/console/apps/create
  2. Name: anything. Category: Chat Bot.
  3. OAuth Redirect URL: http://localhost:4747/auth/callback
  4. Create it, hit Manage, copy the Client ID into the box above.
  5. Click Sign in with Twitch.

Queue rules

Chat posts (the queue list and join confirmations) need Sign in with Twitch — HamQueue can read chat without it, but not speak.
